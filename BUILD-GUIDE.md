# Build guide — in progress

This is an outline for a future “now you can too” guide, not a tested recipe. The current build is documented in DIARY.md; current tasks are in STATUS.md.

## Intended result

A roughly 24-inch-wide front-half Xanathar, convincing at twilight, with posed eyestalks and a fixed mouth. The central eye works as a stationary feature first; motion is optional.

## Proposed sequence

1. Obtain and measure eye blank.
2. Lay out face at full size around the eye.
3. Test one foam-covered brow and its coating/paint.
4. Build a rigid support and accessible eye mount.
5. Print necessary facial forms while doing frame, wiring, and finish work.
6. Fit foam covering, mouth cavity, teeth, and posed stalks.
7. Finish and light the stationary creature.
8. Add and test eye motion if time permits.
9. Rehearse the complete display; retain stationary fallback.
