# Working on Xanathar

## Purpose and constraints

Maintain a practical build diary, checklist, spending record, and eventual reproducible guide. Read README.md, STATUS.md, DESIGN.md, MATERIALS.md, and the latest DIARY.md entry before proposing changes.

The fixed scope is a roughly 24-inch front-half Xanathar, under $200 new spending, for October 31, 2026. The minimum is a convincing stationary prop at twilight. The upgrade is central-eye motion with lighting. Do not reopen discarded full-body, moving-jaw, blink, or animated-tentacle options unless the user requests it.

Optimize hands-on time, money, workspace occupation, and storage. Unattended printing/drying time is a separate resource; use it to reduce manual sculpting. Avoid overplanning. Prefer a small physical test followed by useful fabrication.

## Evidence and updates

- Distinguish user-confirmed facts, photo-based inference, proposals, and physically tested results.
- Never mark purchases, printing, assembly, or tests complete without evidence.
- Record actual spending separately from estimates; unknown cost is not zero.
- After meaningful work, update STATUS.md and append a dated DIARY.md entry. Update DESIGN.md if a decision changes and MATERIALS.md for purchases or costs.
- Preserve old diary entries; add corrections with dates rather than silently rewriting history.
- Dates use America/Chicago. Interim dates are planning targets; Halloween is the actual deadline.
- A deadline checklist is not an active reminder service. Do not claim background monitoring or reminders unless separately configured.
- Do not import unrelated personal details or unreviewed private conversation history into this public repository.

## Fabrication

- Measure the actual eye blank and pipe outside diameter before finalizing mating CAD.
- Check motor identity, voltage/current needs, travel, mechanical clearance, and mounting before selecting power or promising motion.
- Test adhesives and coatings on scrap; do not assume all “foam” or “latex” products are interchangeable.
- Keep the eye mount accessible and provide a stationary fallback.
- State uncertainty in design suggestions; do not call an untested assembly proven.
- Record CAD units, tolerances, source files, print orientation/settings, material use, and actual fit when created.
- No invented STL files, wiring diagrams, test outcomes, or cost totals.

## Documentation style

Use short, concrete instructions and explain why a step matters. Keep the current action list small. Move verified instructions into BUILD-GUIDE.md with links to supporting diary entries and photos. Before calling the guide reproducible, include actual dimensions, BOM, settings, assembly order, finishing recipe, and failure notes.
